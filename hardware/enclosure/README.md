# Enclosure Design and Assembly

## 1. Introduction

The IoT Smart Energy Monitoring System includes a physical enclosure to house and organize its electronic hardware.

The enclosure is an important part of the product development process because it contributes to component protection, hardware organization and overall product integration.

This document records the enclosure-related considerations and the files associated with its development.

## 2. Objectives

The enclosure development aimed to:

- Provide a housing for the electronic assembly.
- Organize the internal hardware.
- Support integration of the custom PCB.
- Provide accessibility for required connections.
- Improve the physical presentation of the prototype.
- Support a more compact product arrangement.

## 3. Enclosure Design Considerations

### 3.1 Component Arrangement

The internal arrangement should accommodate the ESP32, sensing circuitry, PCB and associated connections without unnecessary crowding.

### 3.2 Accessibility

The enclosure should allow access to required interfaces and connections while maintaining appropriate protection.

### 3.3 Ventilation and Thermal Considerations

The enclosure design should account for heat generated by electronic components and the power supply circuitry.

Thermal performance must be evaluated under actual operating conditions.

### 3.4 Mechanical Considerations

The enclosure should provide suitable space for the PCB, connectors and wiring.

Its dimensions and mounting arrangements should be compatible with the assembled hardware.

### 3.5 Electrical Safety

The enclosure must not be assumed to provide electrical protection merely because the circuit is enclosed.

Material properties, insulation, openings, clearances and accessibility of potentially hazardous conductors require appropriate evaluation.

## 4. Assembly Considerations

The general hardware assembly process involves:

1. Preparing the enclosure.
2. Checking compatibility with the PCB.
3. Arranging the electronic components.
4. Organizing internal connections.
5. Providing access to required interfaces.
6. Inspecting the assembled hardware.
7. Performing appropriate functional and safety checks.

The actual assembly sequence may vary depending on the final enclosure design.

## 6. Verification Checklist

The following aspects should be evaluated:

- PCB fitment.
- Component accessibility.
- Wire routing and strain relief.
- Mechanical stability.
- Ventilation and thermal behaviour.
- Protection against accidental contact.
- Suitability of enclosure material.
- Compatibility with the intended installation environment.
