# Future Enhancements

## 1. Introduction

The Smart Energy Monitoring System establishes a foundation for IoT-based electrical energy monitoring. However, several improvements can further enhance its measurement accuracy, reliability, usability and suitability for practical deployment.

This document outlines potential enhancements identified during the development and evaluation of the prototype.

## 2. Measurement Accuracy and Calibration

Future development can focus on improving the accuracy and consistency of electrical measurements.

Proposed improvements include:

- More systematic voltage and current sensor calibration.
- Comparison against calibrated reference instruments.
- Evaluation of measurement errors under different load conditions.
- Improved signal conditioning and noise reduction.
- Periodic calibration verification.
- Better handling of low-load measurements.

## 3. Advanced Power Measurement

The measurement algorithms can be enhanced to support more detailed electrical analysis.

Possible improvements include:

- Improved real-power measurement using synchronized voltage and current sampling.
- More accurate power factor estimation.
- Better handling of nonlinear and varying loads.
- Harmonic analysis.
- Power quality monitoring.
- Detection of unusual electrical behaviour.

These enhancements would require suitable sampling, processing and validation methods.

## 4. Historical Data and Analytics

The cloud-connected architecture provides opportunities for more advanced energy consumption analysis.

Potential enhancements include:

- Daily, weekly and monthly consumption summaries.
- Historical energy consumption graphs.
- Comparison between different time periods.
- Peak consumption identification.
- Consumption trend analysis.
- Downloadable energy reports.

## 5. Mobile Application Improvements

The mobile application can be extended to provide a more informative and user-friendly monitoring experience.

Possible additions include:

- Improved dashboard visualization.
- Customizable consumption summaries.
- Configurable energy usage alerts.
- Monthly electricity cost estimates.
- Historical data comparison.
- Improved navigation and accessibility.

## 6. Energy Usage Alerts

An alert mechanism could help users identify unusual consumption patterns.

Potential features include:

- Configurable power thresholds.
- High-consumption notifications.
- Unusual energy usage alerts.
- Daily or monthly consumption notifications.
- Alerts when communication with the monitoring device is interrupted.

Alert thresholds should be configurable and validated against actual operating conditions.

## 7. Hardware and Product Refinement

Further hardware development can improve the physical implementation and reliability of the system.

Proposed enhancements include:

- PCB layout optimization.
- Improved enclosure design.
- Better component accessibility for maintenance.
- Improved wiring organization.
- Additional hardware protection.
- Evaluation of thermal performance.
- Design optimization for manufacturing.

## 8. Electrical Safety and Reliability

Before practical deployment, the system requires further safety and reliability evaluation.

Future work should include:

- Verification of isolation and protection arrangements.
- Appropriate fuse and overcurrent protection evaluation.
- Assessment of PCB creepage and clearance requirements.
- Enclosure safety evaluation.
- Temperature and long-duration operating tests.
- Testing under different electrical load conditions.
- Review against applicable electrical safety standards.

## 9. Scalability and Connectivity

The system could be extended to support more advanced monitoring arrangements.

Possible directions include:

- Monitoring multiple electrical circuits.
- Centralized monitoring of multiple devices.
- Improved cloud communication reliability.
- Local data buffering during network interruptions.
- Secure device authentication.
- Improved database access control.

## 10. Cost and Manufacturing Optimization

Product-oriented development can also focus on reducing implementation costs without compromising safety or reliability.

Potential activities include:

- Component cost evaluation.
- PCB manufacturing optimization.
- Enclosure cost optimization.
- Component availability assessment.
- Assembly process improvement.
- Evaluation of manufacturing feasibility.

## 11. Conclusion

The current prototype provides a foundation for further development of an IoT-based energy monitoring solution.

Future enhancements will focus on measurement accuracy, electrical safety, historical data analysis, application usability, hardware reliability and manufacturing feasibility.

These improvements can help progress the project from an academic prototype towards a more reliable and practical energy monitoring product.
