# Product Development Journey

## 1. Introduction

The Smart Energy Monitoring System was developed with the objective of creating a practical IoT-based energy monitoring solution.

The development process extended beyond initial prototype implementation to include hardware refinement, sensor calibration, safety considerations, software improvements and product evaluation through stakeholder interactions.

This document records the transition from the initial academic prototype towards a more refined product implementation.

## 2. Initial Prototype Development

The initial development focused on establishing the core functionality of the system.

The major activities included:

- Identifying the need for accessible energy monitoring.
- Selecting suitable voltage and current sensing modules.
- Integrating the ESP32 microcontroller.
- Developing firmware for electrical parameter acquisition and processing.
- Implementing LCD-based local monitoring.
- Establishing Firebase cloud connectivity.
- Developing a mobile application dashboard.

These activities established the foundation for the integrated energy monitoring prototype.

## 3. Hardware Refinement

Following the initial implementation, attention was given to improving the hardware arrangement and overall product integration.

The refinement activities included:

- Reviewing sensor connections and measurement behaviour.
- Improving hardware integration.
- Working on a custom-designed PCB.
- Refining the physical arrangement of electronic components.
- Evaluating enclosure and product assembly considerations.

The objective was to move towards a more organized and practical hardware implementation.

## 4. Sensor Calibration and Measurement Reliability

Measurement reliability was an important area of product improvement.

The voltage and current sensing modules required appropriate calibration to improve the consistency of electrical measurements.

The refinement work involved:

- Reviewing sensor measurement behaviour.
- Adjusting calibration parameters.
- Evaluating measurement consistency.
- Identifying sources of measurement variation.
- Improving the reliability of acquired readings.

These activities highlighted the importance of calibration and verification in developing a dependable energy monitoring solution.

## 5. Electrical Safety Improvements

Electrical safety was considered during product refinement.

The work involved reviewing the hardware arrangement, connections and physical implementation with attention to safe operation.

Important areas included:

- Sensor integration.
- Electrical connection arrangements.
- Hardware protection.
- Physical component organization.
- Enclosure-related considerations.

Further safety evaluation remains necessary before any commercial or permanent installation.

## 6. PCB Development

A custom PCB was developed as part of the product refinement process.

The PCB helped organize the electronic components and their interconnections into a more integrated hardware arrangement.

The development focused on:

- Improving circuit integration.
- Organizing component connections.
- Reducing dependence on loosely arranged prototype wiring.
- Supporting a more compact implementation.
- Improving the physical organization of the product.

The PCB represented an important step towards product-oriented hardware development.

## 7. Software and User Interface Refinement

The software component was further developed to support the overall monitoring experience.

The system integrates:

- ESP32-based embedded firmware.
- Electrical parameter processing.
- LCD-based local visualization.
- Firebase cloud connectivity.
- A mobile application dashboard.

Attention was also given to presenting monitoring information in a manner that users could access and understand.

## 8. PUPA 2026 – Make in BVB

The project was presented at **PUPA 2026 – Make in BVB**, providing an opportunity to evaluate the prototype beyond the academic development environment.

The event enabled interaction with different stakeholders, including:

- Household users.
- Paying guest accommodation owners.
- Hostel students.
- Entrepreneurs.
- Industrial representatives.
- Innovators.
- Mentors.
- Potential customers.

These interactions provided exposure to practical user expectations and different perspectives on energy monitoring solutions.

## 9. Stakeholder Feedback and Product Evaluation

The stakeholder interactions helped identify areas of interest and possible improvements.

The discussions provided insights into:

- The importance of accessible electricity consumption information.
- The usefulness of real-time monitoring.
- The need for understandable mobile dashboards.
- Practical considerations for household deployment.
- Product usability and reliability expectations.
- Opportunities for further product development.

Feedback and observations from these interactions contributed to the continued refinement of the project.

## 10. Product Development Approach

The project followed an iterative development approach.

The general process can be represented as:

**Prototype Development → Testing → Calibration → Hardware Refinement → Software Improvement → Product Presentation → Stakeholder Feedback → Further Refinement**

This approach allowed the development process to incorporate both technical observations and user perspectives.

## 11. Development Outcomes

The product development activities contributed to:

- Improved understanding of sensor calibration.
- Further refinement of hardware integration.
- Development of a custom PCB.
- Greater attention to electrical safety.
- Continued improvement of software integration.
- Exposure to stakeholder expectations.
- Identification of opportunities for future development.

## 12. Current Development Status

The project is at the prototype and product-refinement stage.

Although the project has been presented to stakeholders and received feedback, it should not be considered a commercially validated or mass-produced product.

Further work is required in areas such as:

- Extensive measurement validation.
- Electrical safety evaluation.
- Long-duration reliability testing.
- Product enclosure refinement.
- Cost optimization.
- Field testing.
- Regulatory and compliance evaluation.

## 13. Future Product Direction

Potential future development areas include:

- Improved measurement accuracy.
- Enhanced electrical protection.
- Historical energy consumption analysis.
- Advanced mobile application visualizations.
- Improved product enclosure.
- Optimized PCB design.
- Extended field testing.
- Evaluation of manufacturing feasibility.

## 14. Conclusion

The development of the Smart Energy Monitoring System involved more than implementing an IoT-based monitoring circuit.

Through iterative engineering improvements, PCB development, sensor calibration, software integration and stakeholder interactions at PUPA 2026, the project evolved towards a more refined product concept.

The experience provided exposure to the relationship between technical implementation, product usability, reliability and practical user requirements.n
